## Build

A tool that finds a board could not tell a board that is up from one in recovery, or a flasher
image from either, until it logged in. And recovery served SSH only: a board reached over a USB
cable, by a tool and not a person, said nothing at all.

1. **`state` in the hello document**: `running`, `recovery` (with `recovery_why`), `flasher`
   or `updating`, as two of Orion's own text facts, signed with the rest (no wire change: a
   board that does not say is a board too old to). `hello.state = flasher` for a flasher
   image; `Hello::state()` for whatever updates the board. A program's fact by either name is
   left out. `Node::state()` and `Node::recovery_why()` in the client.
2. **Recovery serves Orion's protocol**: the board's socket and its USB links, with the hello
   document saying `recovery` and why. Nothing from the state directory: the key is one for
   the run.
3. **`last-good.carriers`**: every start that gets as far as serving records its ways in, in
   the settings' words, nothing secret. Recovery with settings that cannot be read opens them
   from it, with security on, and says so.
4. **With neither settings nor record,** the usual socket, and a USB link for any name root
   offers (`UsbLinks::any_name`); anyone else may offer only a named link.

## Built

As above. The shell in recovery is C27's.

Found on the way: a comm core whose start failed after its socket was served left that task
running (a dropped `JoinHandle` detaches, it does not stop), so recovery in the same process
could not have the board's socket. The comm core's tasks are now a `Tasks` that aborts them
when dropped, whether the core stops or its start fails; recovery tries the socket again until
it is free. The carrier enum moved to `carriers.rs` with it, to keep `lib.rs` under 800 lines.

## Proved by

- `crates/comm/hello/tests/hello.rs`: the document says `running` and nothing else of Orion's;
  a program's `hello.state` is left out; `the_document_says_what_the_board_is_doing_and_why_it_is_in_recovery`
  (recovery with its reason, a long reason cut on a character, flasher, updating, running again).
- `crates/comm/link-usb/tests/usb.rs`, `with_no_list_of_links_root_may_hand_over_a_link_by_any_name`:
  a user on the board is refused, root's offer makes the link, a device's session runs over
  it, and the same name again is the same link.
- `crates/comm/comm-app/tests/recovery.rs`, `a_good_start_is_recorded_and_recovery_opens_its_ways_in_and_says_why`:
  a good start says `running` on its socket and records it, with no secret in the record;
  broken settings bring recovery up on the recorded socket saying `recovery` and why; settings
  that cannot be started give recovery on their socket with the comm core's reason; a start
  that fails after its socket is served (an address in use) leaves nothing behind and recovery
  has the socket. With `Tasks` not aborting, that last case fails (`running` is answered).
- `settings.rs`: `hello.state = flasher`, and any other word refused. The flasher example
  sets it and is still read whole by the recovery binary's test.

## Different from the brief

The state is two facts in the existing document rather than new fields: the signature covers
everything before it and is last, so appended fields would have been outside it.
