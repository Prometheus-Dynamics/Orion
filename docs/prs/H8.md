## Built

- `examples/stm32g4-device`: dev's firmware on the new device crates. `CanDevice` over the
  register-level FDCAN1 driver (CAN FD, address 1, ids 0x101/0x181) or `StreamDevice` over
  USART2, with `Topics<2>` for `loop` and `stack`. Standalone (`[workspace]`), no HAL, one
  interrupt vector, stack painting, the `small-mem` byte-loop `mem*`, and `hw-crc` over the
  CRC unit through `orion-wire`'s `crc-extern`.
- `scripts/device-size.sh` keeps the probe budgets and adds the whole firmware: fmt and clippy
  for both links, then flash, static RAM and the stack bound for four variants against
  `examples/stm32g4-device/size-baseline.txt`, 64 B flash and 32 B RAM of tolerance.
  `DEVICE_SIZE_UPDATE=1` rewrites the baseline.
- `scripts/stack-depth.py` from dev, with indirect calls followed from any `orion_` crate.

## Proved so far

The size gate, on the build machine:

| Variant | Flash | RAM in total | dev |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FDCAN (`link-can`, `small-mem`) | 7,040 | 1,132 | 9,228 / 1,988 |
| UART (`link-uart`, `small-mem`) | 6,368 | 1,060 | 8,700 / 1,908 |

RAM in total is 4 B static plus the stack bound (1,128 B for FDCAN).

## Not proved

- **The board run.** See `testing/board-runs/H8.md`: no STM32G4 board is on the bench.
- **`hw-crc`** builds and has never met silicon.

## Different from the brief

- `scripts/device-size.sh` already existed (the probe budgets); the firmware gate was added to
  it rather than replacing it.
- The firmware publishes topics, not dev's record and stream: the new device tier has no
  records or streams. `stack` replaces dev's status values, sampled every second.
- `loop` counts turns per 100 ms, one sample a frame, where dev batched eight 10 ms samples.
