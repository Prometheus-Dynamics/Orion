## Why

I103's gate failed once on
`leases::a_program_that_stops_answering_loses_its_lease_to_a_claimant_and_is_told_by_whom`.
The lease was taken at 1.4996 s, against a `leases.quiet_ms` of 1.5 s. "Never taken before
`leases.quiet_ms`" is what the code promises, and it held only to within about 2 ms:

1. **The comm core** told node logic how long a session had been quiet as
   `now_ms() - heard_ms`. Each of those is rounded down from its own clock, so the difference
   can be up to 1 ms more than had passed.
2. **Node logic** kept `now_ms - quiet_ms` as when the session went quiet, on its own
   whole-millisecond clock. It then took the lease once `now_ms - since >= quiet_ms`. Two
   readings 1 ms apart on that clock may be a hair apart in time: up to 1 ms more again.

The fix is in the code, not the test.

## Built

- **The comm core rounds the time quiet down.** It keeps when it last heard the session at
  the clock's full precision. It tells the services the difference rounded down
  (`quiet_for`), so none is told the session was quiet longer than it was.
  `Service::quiet` says so.
- **Node logic counts its milliseconds since without coming in early.**
  - `Contest` keeps, for each quiet session, when it was told on its clock and how long the
    session had been quiet then (`Told`).
  - At `now_ms`, the session has been quiet at least
    `quiet_ms + (now_ms - at_ms - 1)`, with the last term never below zero. Milliseconds on
    the clock count from the one after the telling, because the first may be a hair long.
  - A claim made in the same millisecond as the telling still counts the whole `quiet_ms`,
    because it comes after the telling.
- **Whole milliseconds still cross.** `NodeQuiet` carries `quiet_ms` as it did, so the wire
  does not change. Node logic's clock stays in milliseconds; it runs through the records and
  resources it is passed to. A comment at each site says which way it rounds.

## The gate's own starvation

I104's second gate went red on three browser tests, which are not in this change. Their cause
was the gate's share of the machine:

- **The cause.** The gate runs as one unit in the user's `app.slice`, with `cpu.weight` 100.
  Other projects' jobs ran as sibling units, so the gate had about 1/N of the machine: it
  started at load 128 on 32 CPUs.
- **The reproduction.** Each test, on 2 CPUs at nice 10 beside 8 busy loops at nice 0, failed
  3 of 3 with the gate's symptoms. Beside the same 8 at nice 10, each passed 3 of 3.
- **The fix.** The coordinator ruled that the gate waits; raising its weight would only move
  the starvation onto the other sessions. The gate's wrapper, outside the repository,
  `scratch/speed/t3/run-gate.sh`:
  - waits to start while `app.slice`'s CPU pressure (some avg60) is 25% or more, checking
    every 30 s, and says so in the gate log;
  - holds at most 60 min, then starts anyway and says so;
  - records the pressure and load it started under.
  The bound comes from today's readings: 67% at load 105, when the browsers failed, and 15 to
  31% at load 60 to 70.
- **The tests themselves** are bounded by usable time (wall time less runnable wait) in I108,
  after I107.

The three are in `docs/flakes.md` with this cause.

## Proved by

Each before and after was run 3 times.

- **`driver::tests::quiet_is_never_said_longer_than_it_was`** (`orion-server`). Heard at
  10.9 ms, now 1510.0 ms: 1499.1 ms passed. Before, 1500 was said, and it failed 3 of 3
  (`1500 ms said for 1.4991s`). After, 1499 is said, and it passed 3 of 3. More times on
  either side of a millisecond's edge are in the test.
- **`contest::tests::a_lease_is_never_taken_before_its_holder_was_quiet_long_enough`**
  (`orion-resources`). Told at 1000 ms that the holder had been quiet 1000 ms. A claim at
  1500 ms may be only 1499.001 ms after it went quiet.
  - Before, the claim was taken, and the test failed 3 of 3 (`too soon`).
  - After, it is refused and a claim at 1501 ms is taken: 3 of 3 passed.
  - Told it had been quiet the whole `quiet_ms`, a claim in that same millisecond is
    taken.
- **The lease integration test** passed 10 of 10 on 2 cores shared with 6 busy loops.
- `orion-resources`, `orion-server` and `orion-node-app` pass with `--all-features`
  (99 tests). Then the gate.
