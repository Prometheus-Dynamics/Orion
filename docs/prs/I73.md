## Why

The device library's `next` is at `bbb3c420`, with its flasher fixes since `a6251ecf`. The
programs that use both Orion and the device library move to it, and Orion moves its pins there
so that all of them land on one pair of revs.

## Built

- **Cargo.toml.** The seven `shm-*` workspace dependencies come from the `.git` URL, at
  `bbb3c42085a5a9afacde7f3c18692a5462c1300f`. `a6251ecf` is an ancestor of that rev.
- **The lockfiles.** Only the `source` lines change. No package is added, removed or moved to
  another version.
- **No byte Orion builds changes.**
  - Nothing under the device library's `shm` directory differs between `a6251ecf` and
    `bbb3c420`, and the seven crates are all there (`shm/sample-time`, `shm/ring`,
    `shm/ring-time`, `shm/ring-doorbell`, `shm/ring-buffers`, `shm/ring-holds`, `shm/fdpass`).
  - The workspace `Cargo.toml` is byte for byte the same at both revs.
- **Nothing else Orion takes from the device library changes.** The 32 files that changed are
  its flasher, slot store and install, host, daemon and its own Orion service, their tests and
  docs. Orion builds none of them. Orion's interface level stays 6.

## Proof
