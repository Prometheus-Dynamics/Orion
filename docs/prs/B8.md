`crates/core/ring-holds`

## Read on dev

- `crates/styx/src/ipc.rs` in Styx (`dev`)

## Build

- Each reader marks what it holds in a page only that reader writes. A buffer is free when no reader marks it.
- A dead reader's marks go away with its page.
- Limits on frames held and on hold time. A reader over a limit gets no more, and a stuck one is cut off.
- The publisher never waits for buffers. It drops frames instead.

## Proof

Stuck, slow and killed readers never starve the others. 0 allocations.
