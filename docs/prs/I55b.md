## Built

Each step's number is now `WALL:CPU`: its slowest seconds, as before, and its slowest CPU seconds
over its whole process tree, as `scripts/bounded.py` counts them since I55. The CPU bound is
three times the CPU number, at least 60, times `GATE_STEP_BOUND_SCALE`, passed as `--cpu`. A
step past it is ended as slow. A step past its wall bound with CPU to spare is reported starved.
The stall bound (I55) and every wall bound are unchanged. A step written without both numbers
refuses to run, and the gate's own refusal check now tries `5`, `5:`, `:5`, `0:5`, `5:0` and
`5:x` beside `x`, empty and `0`.

## The numbers

Each CPU number is the largest of four green gates at ac64643, rounded up:

| Source | Total CPU-s | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Local, incremental | 253 | an ordinary commit |
| Local, fresh target dir, lanes reused | 3,558 | cold workspace builds |
| Local, fresh target dir, `GATE_LANE_REUSE=0` | 4,606 | everything cold |
| CI run 44956669263 | (per part) | CI's machines and caches |

The largest came from the cold runs, apart from CPU-light steps where the numbers are all near
zero (the 60 CPU-s floor bounds those). Two cold runs differed by up to 1.6× on the same step
(size gate 1,812 against 1,276; clippy 370 against 225; load around 65-77), which the factor of
three covers.

## Proof

The gate's `bounded` lifted into a script with a step numbered `1:1`:

- two spinning processes: `TIMED OUT after 44 s, slow: 61.4 CPU-s used, past its bound of 60 CPU-s`;
- one spinning process, which got 42 CPU-s in 60 s at load ~80: `TIMED OUT after 60 s,
  starved: past its backstop of 60 s`.

The refusal check covers the parsing. bounded.py's own cases (I55) cover the bounds.
