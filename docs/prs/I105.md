# I105: V6-LINK-LOCAL-WAY-IN: a board is found and reached over link-local IPv6

**Status:** planned

**Group:** I. Finish

**Wave:** 19

**Needs:** I103

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Why

On a direct cable with no DHCP and IPv4 link-local off, a board's only address may be its
`fe80` one. I100 stopped announcing link-local IPv6, which until then had depended on what
else was built. This PR adds it back on purpose, and makes it reachable.

## To do

- **Listeners on IPv6.** The network way in (9200), SSH and the clock also open on `[::]`
  for the interfaces they are kept to, link-local included.
- **mDNS announces the interface's `fe80` address again.**
  - In every build, with no dependence on features: `orion-mdns` turns on `if-addrs`'
    link-local itself.
  - Only on the interface it was asked on. The receiver takes the scope from the interface
    the answer arrived on.
- **The client connects to `fe80` with a scope** (`orion+tcp://[fe80::…%iface]:9200`), and
  the browse gives the scope.
- **The test.** Two boards in network namespaces, joined by a veth pair, with IPv4 off. One
  finds the other by name and opens a session over `fe80`.
