# I55: A gate step that stops using CPU is ended as stalled within a minute, and each step says its CPU seconds

**Status:** done

**Group:** I. Finish

**Wave:** 19

**Needs:** I57

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Changes

`scripts/bounded.py`, `scripts/gate.sh`, `docs/lock.md`, `TODO.md`.

## Why

A gate step was bounded by wall time alone: three times its slowest green time, at least a
minute. Wall time can't tell a hang from a loaded machine. A hung step was found only at the
end of its bound, which is long for a long step. A step slowed by load and one that had stopped
moving got the same report.

## Built

`bounded.py` reads the step's CPU seconds once a second: every process under it (it is their
subreaper), live or a zombie, with what each waited for, plus what it waited for itself. Only
its own tree is read, so the cost doesn't grow with the machine's process count. Beside the wall
bound, a step can have two more:

- `--cpu N`: past N CPU seconds it is ended as **slow**;
- `--stall N`: having used under 0.1 CPU seconds in N seconds it is ended as **stalled**.

With `--cpu`, a step past its wall bound was still moving and within its CPU, so it is
reported **starved**. Each report gives the wall time and the CPU seconds used. A step that ends
says its CPU seconds.

The gate passes `--stall 60` (times `GATE_STEP_BOUND_SCALE`) to every step. Every wall bound is
unchanged. Each step's CPU bound (`--cpu`, three times its slowest green CPU seconds) is I55b: it
needs those numbers from gates here and in CI, which this change makes print them.

## Proof

`bounded.py --self-test` gains a stalled step, a slow one (a spinning grandchild under a waiting
parent), a starved one and a step that says its CPU. It passes 3 of 3 runs at load ~80, in 3-4 s.
Mutants, 3 runs each, all fail every run:

| Mutant | Fails |
|---|---|
| The watcher never reads | stalled and slow end only at their 100 s wall bound |
| Only what this script waited for counts, not its live tree | slow never ends; the self-test reports it held |
| A wall timeout with `--cpu` is not called starved | starved lacks its report |

Cost: `bounded.py 60 probe -- sleep 20` uses 0.10 CPU-s here against 0.12 before. A first draft
that read every process on the machine twice a second used 1.7.
