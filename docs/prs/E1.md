## Built

- `orion-store` (`crates/node/store`): `Store`, the desired records of one node with their
  hybrid-clock stamps. `put` and `delete` are local writes stamped by the node's clock; `merge`
  takes one version from a peer; `get`, `list`, `since`, `all`, `newest` and `fingerprint` read.
  `restored` starts from what was kept, with the clock past every stamp in it.
- The merge is dev's rule, in one function: the greater stamp wins; on equal stamps a delete
  beats a write, then the larger value. A delete is a tombstone, collected after the retention
  time (7 days); a delete older than that with nothing to delete is ignored. A stamp more than
  the allowed drift (5 minutes) ahead of this node's clock is refused and moves nothing.
- Observed records are separate: each node owns its slice, a peer's slice replaces its last
  whole, and nothing is merged across nodes.
- `take_changes` hands every change, in order, to whoever pushes them to watchers. Left
  untaken past 4,096 they are dropped and the follower is told to read again.
- No lock, no I/O, nothing async: node logic owns the store from one task.

## Proved by

`tests/merge.rs`: dev's rules as tests (last writer for every order of arrival, the tie rules
both ways round, a delete that travels and a later write that brings the record back,
collection and expired deletes, a refused future stamp, a restart with the clock reset), and
the randomized test: 40 seeds, 2 to 5 nodes, 600 steps each of writes, deletes and partial,
reordered, repeated gossip under wall clocks that disagree by up to a minute and step back.
After everyone has heard from everyone, every node holds the same versions at the same stamps,
and they are the newest of everything that was ever written.

## Different from dev

Dev's desired state was a struct of typed sections, with mutation batches, revisions and a
mutation history. Records here are generic: a class, a key, a stamp and bytes the store does
not read, so it merges classes it has never heard of. There is no revision and no history: a
watcher or a peer that is behind asks for everything newer than a stamp.
