## Built

- `crates/gateways/nt4-client-gateway` (`orion-nt4-client-gateway`): the gateway as a
  client of an NT4 server somewhere else. `nt4.server` names it (`host` or `host:port`),
  `nt4.name` is what the board calls itself there. The mapping is F7's: `nt4.publish`
  fields are published on the server, `nt4.accept` topics of the server are published on
  the board, whoever writes them there. Expiry withdraws the publication.
- `orion-nt4-gateway` now has the mapping at work as `Mapped`, with the NT4 side behind
  the trait `Far`: a server's table (F7) or a client's publications (this). `Mapping` is
  the settings both forms share.
- `orion-comm-app` feature `nt4-client` (on by default). `nt4.server` and `nt4.listen`
  together are refused: a board is a server or a client of one.
- `examples/nt4-client-gateway-demo.rs`, `interop/run.sh`, `testing/board-runs/F8.md`.

## Proved by

- [`testing/board-runs/F8.md`](../../testing/board-runs/F8.md): on a CM5 against a
  SystemCore's NT4 server, the board joined, read a topic of the server's, published one
  that a second client was sent with its name, type and values, and left nothing behind.
- `crates/gateways/nt4-client-gateway/interop/run.sh` passes against an ntcore server
  (`pyntcore`), both ways, including a value that changes.
- `cargo test -p orion-nt4-client-gateway`, with a real comm core and the NT4 server of
  `orion-nt4`: the board joins under its name; its field is a `double` on the server that
  follows; two of the server's topics are published on the board as they are and as they
  change; an alarm with a 300 ms expiry is on the server, gone, and back, twice; the
  server is replaced by another on the same port and the board joins it, its field there
  again and the new server's values on the board; a topic that is not mapped never
  reaches the board. Settings: host and port forms, both forms at once refused.
