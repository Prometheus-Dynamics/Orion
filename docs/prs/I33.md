## Fault

CI failed on the I32 push in the step bounds self-test, case "an orphan holding the output". The
leftover was named only by its number (`2711 `) where its name, the marker, was due. A stress run
then found two more races of the same kind in the same self-test. Each assumed something had
happened by a fixed time:

- **The orphan was read mid-`exec`.** The planted step exited the moment it started its orphan,
  so `bounded.py` read the leftovers while the orphan was still going through `setsid`, `bash` and
  `exec -a`. A process in `exec` reads an empty `/proc/PID/cmdline` until its new arguments are
  set. Watching 300 starts, 680 such empty reads were seen.
- **The stop came before the handlers.** "A stop from outside" sent TERM one second after it
  started `bounded.py`. On a loaded machine the interpreter had not yet set its handlers, so TERM's
  default ended it: `exit -15`, no output.
- **The interpreter's start counted against the bound.** A case's time counted from starting the
  interpreter. Under load that start alone took seconds (`took 7.4 s, more than 7 s`), and it is no
  part of the bound being checked.

## Built

- The orphan case's step ends only once its orphan runs as the marker. If it never does, the
  case's bound fails it, visibly.
- Each timed case's process has a name of its own (`<marker>-sleeper`, `-deaf`, `-orphan`,
  `-stop`). Its time counts from when that process runs, and the outside stop is sent then. That
  is after `bounded.py` has set its handlers, because it sets them before it starts the step.
- The wait for a step reads only the tree under that `bounded.py`, through its children lists,
  never every process on the machine. The final check for anything left reads each process's
  arguments once.
- `bounded.py` itself: a leftover whose arguments read empty is read again for up to 100 ms, then
  named by its short name (`[sleep]`). A real gate's report then never names a leftover by its
  number alone. The bounds and the limits each case is held to are unchanged.

## Proof

Under load ~60 to ~119 from other work, 13 self-tests at once:

- **The orphan race, widened.** Forty extra `exec`s (`env env ...`) were planted in front of the
  orphan to widen the gap. The old self-test failed 40 of 208 runs: 33 the orphan, 10 the stop and
  1 the time. The new one failed 0 of 208.
- **The stop race, made certain.** `bounded.py` was made to wait 1.5 s before it set its handlers.
  The old self-test failed 13 of 13 runs (`exit -15`); the new one, 0 of 13.
- Nothing was left running after any run.
- The self-test alone took 3.2 s at load ~119. It took about 2.4 s when it landed in I27, on a
  quieter machine.
