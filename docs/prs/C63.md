# C63: A resource's topic may give a late subscriber its newest values

- **Status:** done
- **Group:** C (client)
- **Wave:** 19
- **Needs:** C29

## Why

C29 let a topic say what a subscriber sent samples as messages gets first: what is published from
now on, or the newest of each field before that. A provider's topics could not say it: `topic`
declared every one `FromNow`, with no expiry. A resource's value that changes rarely, a fan's
mode say, stayed unknown to a dashboard on another board until it next changed.

## Changes

- **`Describing::topic_with(&TopicDecl)`** declares the resource's topic as the `TopicDecl` says,
  its name taken under the resource's (`<resource>.<name>`): history, `expiry_ms`, largest sample
  and `on_subscribe`. A node too old for `Latest` refuses it as `Topics::declare` does, rather
  than quietly starting subscribers from now.
- **`Describing::topic`** is unchanged, and now says so: never stale, from now. It is
  `topic_with` with those two.
- `docs/client.md`, the provider role.

## Proved by

- `orion-provider` test `a_resources_topic_may_give_a_late_subscriber_its_newest_values`. A
  resource declares `mode` with `topic_with` (`Latest`, 5000 ms) and `speed` with `topic`. The
  reader lists both under the resource and reads each declaration: `mode` says `Latest` and
  5000, `speed` says `FromNow` and 0. What a `Latest` topic sends a network subscriber is C29's
  test.
- Not vacuous: with `topic_with` forcing `FromNow`, the test fails on `mode`'s declaration.
