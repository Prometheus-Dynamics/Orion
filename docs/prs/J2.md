# J2: The shared ring crates

**Status:** done

**Group:** J. The stack around Orion

**Wave:** 18

**Needs:** J1

**Runs:** cloud

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Build

- One ring for the whole stack: a standalone family of leaf crates with neutral names (no
  library's name in them), hosted in the device library's repository and depending on nothing
  from it or from Orion. The device library's own crates use it the way Orion does.
- The whole family moves: `ring`, `ring-buffers`, `ring-holds`, `ring-doorbell`,
  `ring-latest`, `ring-time` and `fdpass`. The sample time type becomes its own small crate in
  the family, so `ring` no longer depends on `orion-types`, and J3's `now()` and its docs go
  there. The ring header's version check goes with it.
- The device library does the move and the rename to `SampleTime` in its copy. Orion then
  depends on the family at a pinned revision and drops its copies.

## Proof

The gate, and B9 again on the test board: 10.5 µs per frame or better, no system call from
the publisher.

## Built

- The workspace `Cargo.toml` pins `shm-sample-time`, `shm-ring`, `shm-ring-latest`,
  `shm-ring-time`, `shm-ring-doorbell`, `shm-ring-buffers`, `shm-ring-holds` and `shm-fdpass` by
  git revision (b48efaf of the device library, ring magic `SHMRING1`, pool magic `SHMPOOL1`,
  layout number still 1). Every crate that used Orion's copies uses these.
- Deleted: `crates/core/{ring,ring-buffers,ring-doorbell,ring-holds,ring-latest,ring-time,fdpass}`
  and `crates/testing/ring-model`, whose loom proof, hostile ring and fuzz target are the
  family's own (`shm-ring-model`). The `ring` fuzz target went with it.
- `orion_types::SampleTime` is `shm_sample_time::SampleTime`: one type for the whole stack.
  `orion-types` lost its `now` feature; `now()` is there on any Unix target.
- `orion-ring-bench` stays in Orion, built on the family: it is Orion's acceptance run.
- The boundary check no longer allows `unsafe` in the deleted crates, and does not read a
  manifest's `git = "..."` address as a mention of a product.
- `docs/ring.md`, `docs/design.md` and `AGENTS.md` say where the rings are now.

## Proved by

- The gate, on the family.
- B9 again on the test board (`testing/board-runs/B9.md`, "Again, on the shared ring crates"):
  no system call from the publisher, no allocation, every frame received. Built from before and
  after the move and run in turn, the two are the same: frame medians 11.1, 10.5 and 12.9 µs
  against 11.2, 11.2 and 14.2 µs, and 471 against 470 ns for a whole frame on one thread.

## Different from the brief

- The brief's bar of 10.5 µs per frame is met in one run of five. The other runs are 10.8 to
  12.9 µs, and Orion's own copies, measured beside them, are the same or slower. The bar is from
  before hold marks gained their version word, and this board's median moves by 3 µs from one
  run to the next. The move itself costs nothing measurable.
- `orion-measure` stays Orion's: it holds the clock calls `crates/comm/*` uses and the cost
  counters, which the family does not carry.
