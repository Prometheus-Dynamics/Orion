## Built

- `crates/node/resources` (`orion-resources`): `Resources` (`check`, `wrote`, `gone`,
  `forget_sessions`) for who provides what, and `list`, `find`, `holder`, `bind`, `release`,
  `release_all` over the store.
- `orion-node-app`: a `Put` is checked entry by entry before any entry is stored, and a
  session that ends has its resources marked lost and its watchers told.

## Proved by

- `crates/node/node-app/tests/resources.rs`, through the `orion` client against a real comm
  core: a camera driver describes a camera with a label, a topic and an action. Another
  program lists it with all of those. It is bound by a lease; a second holder is `busy`. A
  third program can neither overwrite it nor delete it. The driver's session ends: a watcher
  is told it is `Lost`, it is still listed with its topics, and its lease stands. The driver
  comes back and it is `Ready`. The driver withdraws it: it is gone and so is its lease.
- `crates/node/resources/tests/resources.rs`, 12 tests: listing, one holder, a lease that
  ends, what cannot be bound, one provider, records that are not this node's resources, lost
  and back, a lost resource nobody holds, withdrawal, leases written as records, releasing
  all of a holder's, and the comm core going.

## Different from the brief and from dev

- "Bindings from workloads to resources": the binding exists, as a lease with the workload as
  holder, with its rules. What a workload needs is not on the wire: `WorkloadPut` has labels
  a node must have and no list of resources. E9 (placement and leases) is where a workload's
  needs become leases, and it will have to say where the need is written.
- Dev had resource records with capacities, endpoints, ownership modes and a separate binding
  table reconciled each tick. Here a resource is one record and a binding one lease.
- After node logic restarts it did not know which session provides which resource until the
  provider wrote again, so a provider that died in between was not marked lost. Closed
  afterwards: the comm core tells sessions that node logic is back (`NodeBack`), clients write
  their resources again, and unclaimed ones are lost after two seconds
  (`crates/testing/drills/tests/providers.rs`).
