# C59: A client over a link says hello again until it is welcomed

- **Status:** done
- **Group:** C (client)
- **Wave:** 19
- **Needs:** C57

## Why

Over a link, the client said hello once, waited one timeout and reported `TimedOut`. A link
that is not signed loses frames with nothing said, so a hello lost once meant one long wait,
then a failure. That breaks "a way in never gives up" for exactly the transport the boards use.

## Changes

- `orion-connect` `hello.rs` (new, so `lib.rs` stays under 800 lines):
  - Over a link that is not signed, the hello is said again while there is no welcome: after
    250 ms (`FIRST_AGAIN`), the wait doubling to 2 s (`MOST_BETWEEN`), until `options.timeout`.
    This is the default, not an option, for `over`, `over_link` and `reconnect_over`.
  - It is safe because a hello inside a session on a link starts over (C57).
- A welcome arriving during a session, the answer to a hello said again, becomes the session's
  `welcomed()`: it is the session the board now has.
- A `Reject` during a session is `ClientError::Lost`: the comm core has no session for this one
  (`NoSession`, C57), and the caller says hello again.
- `Session::reconnect_over_with(open, keep_trying)` is the way back that never gives up. It
  opens new halves and says hello over them, again and again at the pace of `reconnect`, until
  it is welcomed or `keep_trying` says stop.
- `reconnect_over` now lets the old halves go before it says anything over the new ones, as it
  was documented to. Before, the old reading thread could take the welcome.
- The connection tests are split: the board harness is in `tests/common`, and the link tests
  are in `tests/link.rs`.
- `docs/client.md`.

## Proved by

`tests/link.rs`:

- `a_hello_lost_on_the_link_is_said_again_until_it_is_welcomed`: one hello is lost.
- `hellos_lost_again_and_again_are_said_again_on_a_capped_backoff`: four are lost, and the
  welcome comes no sooner than the backoff allows.
- `a_welcome_that_comes_late_is_followed_by_the_session_the_board_now_has`: the first welcome is
  read only after the hello was said again; the second becomes the session, and calls work.
- `a_link_that_stays_silent_is_tried_again_with_new_halves_until_it_answers`: the first halves
  lose everything; the second try is welcomed, after one wait.
- `a_link_plugged_in_again_says_hello_again_as_the_same_program`: `reconnect_over` twice on the
  same link.

Mutations, each failing:

- no hello said again (the loss tests);
- a late welcome not taken;
- the old halves not let go first.

Seen once under load and not repeated in three runs: `alive.rs`
`a_comm_core_silent_on_the_boards_own_socket_is_kept_and_its_hang_up_is_heard` failed its
connect with a reset. It goes on the load sweep's list.
