## Built

- `orion-discovery`: `Discovery::start(config, backend, on_event)`. The node announces
  `_orion._tcp` with its name, key, cluster, wire version and `orion+tcp` port, keeps the peers
  it has seen until a goodbye or their time runs out, and tells the caller when an enrolled peer
  is present, has moved, or has gone.
- Trust is asked of `orion-keys`, never kept here: discovered, enrolled, revoked, key mismatch,
  incompatible. Finding a peer never makes it trusted.
- With a shared enrollment key in the keys, the node runs the asking side of
  `orion_keys::enroll` with every discovered peer over `orion+tcp`, requiring the peer to hold
  the key it announced, and retries from 5 s doubling to 5 minutes.
- Backends: `MemoryDiscoveryBus` (tests, and embedders that find peers another way) and, behind
  feature `mdns`, `MdnsDiscoveryBackend` on the `mdns-sd` crate as on dev.

## Proved by

`tests/discovery.rs`: two comm cores on a bus in memory, with real `orion+tcp` on loopback, find
each other, enroll with the shared key, and connect as peers known by name; a third without the
key is found and trusted by neither; a wrong key never enrolls. Trust states, a moved address, a
goodbye and a silent peer's expiry are covered under paused time.

`real_mdns_comm_cores_find_each_other_and_enroll` is ignored by default because it needs
multicast. Run by hand on the build machine with
`cargo test -p orion-discovery --features mdns real_mdns -- --ignored`: passed, both enrolled in
2.4 s and the goodbye was heard.

## Different from dev

- The TXT layout is version 2: `cp` (control protocol version) and the HTTP ports are gone with
  the things they named; `wv` and `rev` carry the wire version and interface revision.
- The mDNS thread no longer wakes every 250 ms to look at a stop flag. It sleeps until something
  is heard or the browse is due again, and stopping shuts the daemon down, which ends the wait.
- The discovery task has no periodic tick: it sleeps until the next expiry or enrollment retry,
  and with nothing listed, until something is announced.
- Dev's enrollment hello carried the initiator's URL so the responder could sync back. The
  responder here learns the initiator's address from the initiator's own announcement.
- Private-link trust without a shared key (`docs/discovery.md` on dev) is not carried: it was a
  rule for security off, where next signs nothing and trusts by carrier.
