# I108: The browser tests bounded by usable time, not wall time

**Status:** planned

**Group:** I. Finish

**Wave:** 19

**Needs:** I107

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Why

On a shared machine a test's wall-clock bound measures the other projects' load too. The three
browser tests failed at their 90 s and 120 s bounds while the gate was starved of CPU (I104,
`docs/flakes.md`). I104 makes the gate wait for the machine. This makes the bounds count only
time the test could use.

## To do

- Bound the browser tests (`ws` `browser.rs`, `web-tests` `video.rs` and `client.rs`) by usable
  time: wall time since the test started, less the time its threads spent runnable but waiting
  (`/proc/<pid>/task/<tid>/schedstat`). This is the workspace's per-test bound in
  `docs/gate-speed.md`, from the workspace skeleton.
- The limits stay as they are. An outer wall limit of ten times the normal one stops a test that
  never gets a CPU. The report says which it was: slow, stalled or starved.
- Self-test it the way the notes say, asserting on usable time, never on wall time.
