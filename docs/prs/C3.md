## Build

- Declare, open and subscribe. Makes rings, hands them out once.
- One cap on total ring memory for the board.
- A restarted comm core accepts rings re-offered by their publishers.
- A publisher going away is visible to its subscribers.

Two kinds were added to the wire for this, at the end of the topics range (new kinds, so no
recorded vector changed): `TOPIC_BELL` hands a publisher one subscriber's doorbell or takes it
back, and `TOPIC_STATE` tells a subscriber where its topic is (waiting for a publisher, live,
publisher gone). The subscriber owns its doorbell and sends it with its subscribe, so a restarted
comm core has nothing to recreate. A subscriber is also sent the topic's declaration before its
ring, which is how a tool learns a topic's fields.
