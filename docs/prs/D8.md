## Built

- `orion-provider`: `Provider::resource(name, kind)` starts describing a resource; `.topic(..)`
  declares `<resource>.<name>` and returns its publisher; `.action(..)` registers
  `<resource>.<name>` with its policy; `.publish(state)` writes the record. A `Resource` can
  change its state, serve its actions, and be withdrawn.
- The reading side is `resources(records, prefix)`: each resource with its kind, node, state,
  topics, actions and labels.
- `orion-msg-records` gained `ResourceDescription`: the topics, actions and labels a provider
  writes into a resource record's `attributes`. It is a convention, not a new kind: a record
  whose attributes are something else is still found, with nothing listed.
- `orion-stubs` (`crates/testing/stubs`): a real comm core in a thread with stand-ins for the
  store, the action router, the file receiver and the workload tracker, for the client tests
  from here on.

## Proved by

`tests/provider.rs`: a fan's provider declares a topic of two fields and an action. A reader
written against the six concepts only, with no mention of fans, lists the resource and prints
its fields with their names, values and units from the declaration; runs the action it found
in the record; and prints the new values. The expected output is in the test.
