## Build

- One writer, many readers, in a memfd. `docs/ring.md` pins the layout.
- Entries hold sequence, time, flags, field, length, payload.
- Readers detect torn and overwritten entries, and bounds-check every offset.
- A slow reader skips ahead and counts what it missed.
- Subscriber count in the header.

## Proof

0 allocations and no system calls on publish. Tests with a hostile writer and hostile offsets.
