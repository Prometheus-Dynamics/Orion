# J1: Opaque data: sample times and buffer topics

**Group:** J. The stack around Orion

**Wave:** 17

**Needs:** none

**Runs:** cloud

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Build

- Orion moves bytes with a name, a time and a place in memory. Nothing in it knows what a
  frame, an image or a pose is, and a new kind of data never needs an Orion change. Written
  into the design as a rule.
- `FrameTime` becomes `SampleTime`; "frames are topics" becomes buffer topics; example topic
  names say nothing about cameras. A wire frame (`[version][kind][seq][payload][crc]`) keeps
  its name: that is the protocol's word, not data's.

## Proof

The gate. No `FrameTime` left; the design states the rule.
