## Build

The device library's `shm-*` crates changed after 0aa30510: a stalled reader loses its oldest
unread frames, readers stamp the frames they took, and new calls for consumers that fill buffers.
Every consumer of the crates meets at one revision, d6b9361d, so Orion moves there in the
same release as the other consumers, and checks that nothing it relies on moved.

## Built

The eight `shm-*` workspace dependencies at `d6b9361d006e5f633149b6dea8bb5d1be9483d0a`.

What changed under Orion, from `git diff 0aa30510 d6b9361d -- shm`:

- additions only in the calls Orion makes: `Holders::taken` and `Marker::taken` with defaults,
  `BufferWriter::taken`, `Reader::attach_from_start`, the ring's end word and
  `BufferReader::end`, `Pool::mapped` and writable pool views, `Doorbell::subscriber_fds`;
- one layout change: a reader's marks page holds a taken stamp per buffer after the marks, twice
  the words. Orion makes marks pages only with `Marks::create` (the client's buffers, the tests,
  the bench), and the publisher's table checks the size against the same crate, so it follows.

Also, from a consumer's NT4 test: `docs/comm.md` says the order NT4 announces topics declared together
is not defined.

## Proved by

- The ring, buffer and adopt tests (`orion-buffers` with `adopt.rs` and `frames.rs`,
  `orion-broker-buffers`, `orion-broker`, `orion-topics`), and the gate.
- B9 on the test board, twice (`testing/board-runs/B9.md`, "Again, at d6b9361d"): 9.0
  and 11.4 µs from publish to a subscriber holding the frame, no system call, no allocation, no
  frame dropped. The reader's stamp costs about 25 ns a frame.

## Different from the brief

Found on the gate: `orion-ctl`'s `node time` test wanted the measured offset under 1 ms against
the same clock, and a loaded machine measured 1.6 ms over a 3.4 ms round trip. It now holds the
offset to the round trip, which is all the measurement promises.


There was no brief: the move was asked for by the consumers meeting at one revision.
