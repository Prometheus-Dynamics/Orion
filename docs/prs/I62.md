## Fault

I60's fault, on a CAN bus. A bus reassembles frames of at most 4096 bytes (`MAX_FRAME`), but its
connections said they had no limit. A device that said it takes more was welcomed with more, and
a frame larger than 4096 was sent and then dropped by the reassembler. The device heard nothing.

## Built

- **The limit is reported.** `CanConnection::max_frame` is `Some(4096)`. From there, I60 does
  the rest:
  - the session's `max_frame` is clamped to 4096, and the welcome says so;
  - an answer too large is refused `buffer_too_small`;
  - a hello too large says its size and the limit.
- **Nothing on the wire changes.** Interface level 6.

## Proof

- **New test:** `tests/hello.rs`,
  `a_hello_too_large_for_the_bus_is_refused_saying_its_size_and_the_limit`. It runs on the
  in-memory bus of `tests/bus.rs`, with the comm core's broker and hello service, so it needs
  no `vcan` interface.
  - A device that says it takes 64 KiB is welcomed with 4096.
  - Its small hello comes.
  - After a program on the board publishes five 1000-byte facts, the device's hello is refused
    `buffer_too_small`, with a size over 5000 and the limit 4096.
- **Mutant:** the limit back at `None` fails the test 3 of 3, at the welcome.
- The four existing bus tests pass unchanged.
- **The landing gate passed.**
