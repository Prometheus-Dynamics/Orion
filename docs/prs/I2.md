# I2: Graph bridge

**Status:** dropped

**Group:** I. Finish

**Wave:** 10

**Needs:** D2, D3

**Runs:** cloud

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Build

- Topic source and sink nodes as a plugin for the graph runtime.
- A buffer topic comes in as a resident the owner's adapter can turn into its own type, with no copy.

## Proof

A graph reads and writes topics in the graph runtime's daemon.

## What happened

It was built (540ce63 on `next`, in `crates/bridges/`): a plugin with three nodes, a
topic reader, a topic writer and a frame reader that handed the graph the publisher's own
buffer, with tests against the runtime's daemon. Then it was dropped: a bridge belongs with
the library it plugs into, and Orion keeps no product names. Orion's side of it is the
ordinary client; the runtime can bring the bridge back on its side. Its history is in git.
