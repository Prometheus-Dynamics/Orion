## Built

- `crates/comm/ssh-forward` (`orion-ssh-forward`): `connect`, a connection from the board
  to a name or an address, given up after ten seconds, and `carry`, its bytes on a channel
  both ways until either end closes.
- `orion-ssh` feature `forward`: a client that is logged in may open a `direct-tcpip`
  channel (`ssh -L`, `ssh -W`). The connection is made in a task of its own, so the rest of
  the client's connection goes on meanwhile. `ssh.forward = false` refuses them.
- `orion-comm-app` feature `ssh-forward` (on by default); setting `ssh.forward` (`true`).

## Proved by

`cargo test -p orion-ssh --features forward --test forward`, with OpenSSH's `ssh`:

- `ssh -W`: what is typed reaches a service only the board reaches and its answer comes
  back; a megabyte goes through whole.
- `ssh -N -L`: a port on the client's machine is the service; two connections through the
  one tunnel at once each get their own answers.
- Nothing listening at the other end, and a client that is not let in, get no connection.
- With `ssh.forward` off the client is told forwarding failed, and still gets a shell.

`cargo test -p orion-ssh-forward`: a connection by address and by name; nothing listening,
a port that is not one and a name of nowhere are each refused with the reason.

## Different from the brief

- No list of where a connection may go: whoever may log in may reach what the board
  reaches, as they could from a shell.
- Remote forwarding (`-R`), agent and X11 forwarding are not there, as in dev's note.
