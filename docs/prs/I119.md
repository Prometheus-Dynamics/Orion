# I119: The lane keys' self-test runs beside the tests

**Status:** dropped: no gain on CI, taken back by [I120](I120.md)

**Group:** I. Finish

**Wave:** 19

**Needs:** I118

Read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/design.md` first.

## Why

What a push waits for on CI. `gate (main)` finishes last on almost every push: a median 231 s
of job wall time, a 193 s main step, over the last 20 runs. On I118's run that step was tests,
120 s, after the lane keys' self-test, 58 s, which ran alone before them. Here the self-test
is stamped when its scripts pass and seldom runs. On CI every checkout is fresh, so it ran on
every push.

## Changes

- **`beside LABEL WALL:CPU COMMAND...` in `scripts/gate.sh`**: a step of the main part run
  beside the rest of it, on CI's main runner too, where the lanes are runners of their own.
  It is joined with the lanes and fails the gate as a lane does. The lane keys' self-test
  runs so, beside the tests. It takes its keys in a copy and a target of its own, so it
  shares no cargo lock with them. It checks the same things.
- **Its bounds are for running beside the tests: 60 s and 30 CPU-s**, backstops 180 s and 90
  CPU-s. They were 25:12, set for running alone. Alone, it takes 27 to 47 s and 14 to 15 CPU-s
  here (load 130 to 144, nice 10), and 58 s and 27.8 CPU-s on CI.

  The first gate of this change was red. Beside the tests, at load 82 rising to 139, it had
  11.9 CPU-s in its 75 s backstop, still moving, and was stopped as starved. That run was
  reported, not run again; the bounds are the fix.
- **`scripts/gate-self-test.sh`** has the cases:
  - a red step beside fails the gate, and a green one passes and is timed;
  - a sleeping hang run beside, with the lane keys' self-test's own bounds read from
    `scripts/gate.sh`, still ends red, found by the stall bound (made 2 s for the test),
    whatever the wall bound;
  - its CPU backstop is held at 90 CPU-s at most. A spinner past a CPU bound is planted in
    `scripts/bounded.py --self-test` (`a slow one`).

## Proved by

- **The gate's self-test** passed 3 of 3. With `beside` never joined, it failed 3 of 3: a red
  step beside passed.
- **The bounds:** with its CPU bound at 31 (93 CPU-s), the gate's self-test failed 3 of 3.
