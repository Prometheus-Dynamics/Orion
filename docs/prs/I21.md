## Build

After a change to a crate that the shipped files hold (76 of the 92), the size gate ends last.
Each of its nine builds ends in a fat-LTO link of one thread: 38.6 s of the 45 s that `orion`
for this machine takes alone after an `orion-check` change. Built beside the other lanes,
the same build took 123-181 s. The other lanes (feature matrix, other systems, device size) and
the main steps had finished well before it, yet took processors from those links while they
ran.

## Built

- The feature matrix, other-systems and device-size lanes run at `nice` 10. The size gate, the
  main steps (clippy, tests) and the small device steps (mcu template, packaging) keep their
  priority. The same steps run, in the same lanes.

at the lowest load. With the other lanes behind, the gate's time spreads 66-101 s instead of
64-192 s. Every gate passed.
