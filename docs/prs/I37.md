## Fault

CI's run of I36 failed its tests step. All 900 tests passed, but
`orion-files-pull-many-…` was left in the step's TMPDIR. Three local gates had passed with
nothing left.

`a_fetch_says_how_far_it_is_and_only_so_many_run_at_once` ends by starting one more fetch,
to show there is room again, and then returned at once. The fetch runs on a thread of its own
and makes its `.part` file in the test's directory. When that happened while the test's
`Scratch` was being removed, the directory was not empty at the end and stayed.

## Built

The test steps the fetches until none is running before it returns. A fetch reports only after
its file is closed. Nothing the test checks changes.

## Proof

The test alone, 100 runs each, each with a TMPDIR of its own looked at after the run:

| Test | Passed | Left something |
|---|---|---|
| Before | 100 of 100 | 68 of 100 |
| After | 100 of 100 | 0 of 100 |

Recorded in `docs/flakes.md`.
