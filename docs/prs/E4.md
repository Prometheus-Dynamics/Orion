## Built

`crates/node/workloads` (`orion-workloads`): `Tracker`, with `put`, `remove`, `report`,
`executor_up`, `executor_down`, `tick`, `next_deadline`, `take_commands`, `take_changes`;
`Workload` (an owned `WorkloadPut`); `RestartConfig`. No I/O and no clock: E5 owns one and
carries its commands. The rules are in `docs/node.md`, "The workload tracker".

## Proved by

`tests/tracker.rs`, 42 tests, by state: nothing there, Pending, Starting, Running, Stopping,
Gone and Failed at rest, Failed waiting, Lost, and an executor's inventory. In each state,
each of put (same, new content), remove, force remove, every report, the executor going, the
executor coming with it, without it and with it ended, and time passing. Among them:

- A removed run stays Stopping through any amount of time, and through its executor going and
  coming, until it is reported gone or the executor is back without it.
- The waits are 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 60, 60 … seconds for twelve failures in a row, with no
  giving up; with `max_attempts` 2 it gives up after two.
- An executor that comes back with nothing has its workloads started at once under every
  policy, with the count of failures untouched.

## Different from dev

- Dev planned by diffing desired against observed state each tick, across the runtime, the
  node app and the control plane, and bound resources and leases in the same pass. Here the
  tracker is driven by events and does only runs; placement and leases are E9.
- Dev had no Stopping or Lost and dropped a record when asked. The record now stays.
- Dev's restart was the executor's. Here it is Orion's, since an executor keeps no journal.

## For E5 to E7

- An executor is "here" for the tracker only from `executor_up`, which takes the inventory:
  call it when `ExecutorInventory` arrives, not at `ExecutorOffer`, so that adoption comes
  before any start.
- `Reported::Orphan` and the names `executor_up` returns are runs to stop on that executor.
- E6 (stop that takes too long) and E7 (files before start) have no hook yet: Stopping has no
  deadline, and `Start` is issued as soon as an executor is here.
