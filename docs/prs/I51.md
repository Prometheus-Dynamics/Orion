## Why

The programs that take Orion and the device library together move to the device library's
`next` at `0d37cc55`. Orion moves its pins there first, so they all land on one pair of revs.
A later pin move is expected once more device library work is in.

- **The lockfiles.** Only the `source` lines change: no package was added, removed or moved to
  another version. Every crate under the device library's `shm` directory, and its workspace
  `Cargo.toml`, is byte for byte the same at `c65d234f` and `0d37cc55`, so no byte Orion builds
  changes.
