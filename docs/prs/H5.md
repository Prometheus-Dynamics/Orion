## Built

- `crates/comm/device-gateway` (`orion-device-gateway`):
  - `Watch<S>` wraps a service and notes the topics and actions a `device` session offered
    through it, once the service took them; a session that closes marks its device lost.
  - `DeviceGateway` keeps one entry per device, by hello name. A new session of the same
    device starts its offers over.
  - The gateway's own provider session, in memory, started with the first device (a board
    with none has no extra session and nothing that pings): hello, pings, and a `Put` of the device's
    `ResourceRecord` (kind `device`, its topics and actions in the description) whenever it
    changed. A record that was refused is written again a second later.
- `orion-comm-app`: feature `devices` (default), which wraps the broker and the relay.
- `orion-relay`: `ActionRegister`, `ActionProgress`, `ActionResult`, `FileSink` and
  `FileStatus` admit devices as well as programs on the board.
- `orion-stubs`: the board has the gateway, and `Board::start_for_node_with_links` serves
  serial links.
- `crates/testing/device-tests` (`orion-device-tests`): the end-to-end test.

## Proved by

`crates/testing/device-tests/tests/devices.rs`: a firmware built from the device crates runs on
one end of a pseudo-terminal; the board serves the other end as a serial link, with node logic.
An ordinary client finds `imu-board` as a resource of kind `device` with its topic and action,
subscribes to the topic and reads samples the device publishes only once someone listens, runs
the action and gets its result. The device goes: its resource is `Lost` with its offers kept.
It comes back: `Ready` again. Passed five runs in a row.

## Different from the brief

- No separate host session code. Links (C11, C12) already bring a device into the comm core as
  a session, and the broker and node logic already answer what it sends; what was missing is
  the resource, so that is what the gateway is.
- A device appears once it has declared or registered something: the server tells services
  about frames and closed sessions, not about hellos.
- A device could not register actions or answer runs through the relay at all (the routes
  admitted programs on the board only). Fixed here.
- Time sync for a device's samples is not done: a sample carries the device's time as given.
