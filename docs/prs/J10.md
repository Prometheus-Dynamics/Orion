## Built

`docs/lock.md`. The lock revision is the commit that adds it. It holds:

- the wire facts: revision 1, level 2, the shared-memory crates at `bded77a0` (layout 2);
- the break list from `455a956e`, by part:
  - the wire (appended fields and new kinds, nothing an older side misreads);
  - the error codes 20, 21 and 22, with what each older session is told;
  - the hello document;
  - the Rust client (`Refused` with its words, `Welcomed`, `node_key()`, the new `ClientError`
    cases, `#[non_exhaustive]`);
  - `Service` and `Connection` implementations;
  - node logic's lease rules;
  - the comm core;
  - shared memory;
  - microcontrollers;
  - the web client;
  - what is unchanged (`orion.h`);
- the level table;
- the known limits:
  - "with security on, a lease holder is not yet bound to a principal (C32)";
  - only the network way in tries again;
  - every build takes the shared-memory crates from the device library's repository, and the
    client needs nothing of the device library at run time;
  - armv7 and riscv64 board files have not run on a board;
- the builds the gate checks at this revision.

## Proved by

  - the old `ClientError` and `Welcomed` were compared with the new;
  - the `orion-server` traits, `orion.h`, `orion_mcu.h`, the device crates and the web client
    were diffed.
