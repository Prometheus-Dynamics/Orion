## Why

CI's run of I72's push (45085152871, gate (main)) failed in
`ctl` `fetch.rs` `file_fetch_says_a_stall_waits_it_out_and_gives_up_only_when_asked`. The fetch
without a limit ended without first saying `no progress for 5 s … still waiting`.

The program in that test went quiet for 5.6 s from when it first saw that the client had some
of the file. The client says it is quiet after 5 s with no progress. But up to a window of
chunks (8 of 32 KiB) was still on its way when the program went quiet, and those arrived during
the quiet. The client's quiet began only when the last of them was in, so a runner that took
over 600 ms for them left the client never 5 s quiet.

This is the third entry for this test in `docs/flakes.md`. The shape was wrong, not the
margin: a quiet timed from the program's first sight of progress is not the client's quiet.

None of I69, I70 or I71 changed what is on its way. A fetch passes through the comm core's
`file-offers` service, not the relay. Its window (`FETCH_WINDOW`, 8) and chunk size are as
they were, and so is the outbox: 256 KiB before I69 too, which only named it.

## Built

- **The `ctl` test.** The program goes on when it is told to, not after a time of its own.
  The first fetch tells it once the tool has given up. The second tells it when the tool has
  printed "still waiting", through a writer that watches what the tool prints. The program's
  quiet then lasts until the client has seen it, however long the chunks on their way took to
  arrive.
- **The same shape elsewhere**, in `file-offers` `quiet.rs`:
  - `a_quiet_program_is_said_to_be_stalled_and_the_fetch_waits_it_out` resumed the program
    900 ms after its quiet began, and wanted three stalls of 100 ms before that. It now
    resumes after 900 ms and once three stalls have been said.
  - `a_fetch_given_a_limit_gives_up_on_a_quiet_program_saying_so` counted its 3 s hang guard
    from the program's quiet. It now counts it from the client's last progress.
- **Looked at and left:**
  - `paused.rs`: its pauses are about the program's own session timeout, and nothing it checks
    waits on the client's quiet; its second test already waits until all of the window is in.
  - `files.rs`'s slow source pauses while hashing, before anything is sent.
  - The test board's held commits hold from the commit, after the last chunk.
- **`docs/flakes.md`** has the row.

## Proof

A delay planted at the client, in `crates/client/files/src/fetch.rs` where a chunk of a gift
arrives, makes what is on its way take longer than the margin. Three runs each, each through
`scripts/bounded.py`, the test binary's hash checked to be the one built with the plant:

| Test | Planted | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ctl` `fetch.rs` stall test | 120 ms before every chunk | fails 3 of 3, at `fetch.rs:143`, 1 line where 2 were due, as CI did | passes 3 of 3 |
| `quiet.rs` stalled test | 150 ms before each chunk in the first 300,000 bytes | fails 3 of 3: `said stalled each 100 ms of the quiet: []` | passes 3 of 3 |

Without the plant, all three tests pass 3 of 3. The give-up test passes with the plant before
and after: its 3 s guard was not reached by these delays, and it is fixed for the shape alone.
**The landing gate passed.**
